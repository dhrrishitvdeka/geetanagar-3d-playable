How this was built

The 1,449 building outlines and every road are traced from Google Maps' top view; the field and park outlines from satellite imagery, and the footpaths, divider, railings and park fence from Street View (Mar 2026). Hill shapes come from Google's terrain layer, with Faculty School set about 38 m above the road. Building heights and colours are estimates.
